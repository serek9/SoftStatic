# SoftStatic: Handoff

How to pick the project up on another computer. **PLAN.md §8 Project Status is the authoritative status** (phases, architecture, decisions, known issues, baselines, next task) and is updated every phase. The status sections below are a snapshot from 2026-10-30, after Phase 46 (small menus, the fifth of the tackle, company and living-lake Phases 41–50 of [PLAN_TACKLE.md](PLAN_TACKLE.md)), on the branch `claude/charming-goodall-hvh07h`.

## State in one paragraph

Phases 1 to 12 of [PLAN.md](PLAN.md) are built, checked and committed, one commit per phase, and pushed to `origin/main`. **Milestone A** (the cozy loop, Phase 7) and **Milestone B** (the liminal slice on Fogbound, Phase 10) are reached. On Fogbound Lake you sail, cast, watch the lure sink, hook and fight a fish, and sell or keep it. A HUD shows the clock, coins and bait. P pauses (volume, mute, save export/import), J opens the field journal, M opens the map, which leads to the bait shop and the cabin. At night, with unease, the water reflects things the shore does not have, the dog stares at a hidden spot, an owl calls at 02:47 and 03:33, and journal pages gain hints after you leave them. The map now opens three more waters: Blackwater Creek (300 coins; a current carries the lure), The Pines (1500; a traffic light that blinks faster near the owl's hours, a diner, a parking lot, the same pier as Fogbound) and the Red Room Reservoir (6000 plus a catch made in a clock window; curtains, a zigzag shore, a crimson reflection, and structures that move after every cast). None of the three has fish yet (Phase 14). TAB turns on passive mode: the boat fishes by itself at about 60% of active income. Time away is fished the same way when you come back (up to 8 hours), and a VHS-style tape shows what was caught, sometimes with `???` lines, a missing stretch, or a fish in your own handwriting that you never caught. `?compact` opens a small view for a second monitor. Since then the improvement audit ([SOFTSTATIC_IMPROVEMENT_AUDIT.md](SOFTSTATIC_IMPROVEMENT_AUDIT.md)) has been built in batches: a keyframed dusk sky with a moon, stars and clouds, fog that drifts and thickens, light on the water, gear shown on the boat, Phase 13's audio (`js/audio/engine.js`), feedback on bite, hook and landing, petting the dog (S or ↓), a HUD that fades when idle (H hides it), ambient life, a drizzle that comes with unease, and the map no longer sells waters that have no fish yet. Phase 14 is done too (**Milestone C**): 36 species across the four waters, the Red Room opening to whoever catches the Pines' 03:33 fish, final balance, touch controls with on-screen buttons, a performance audit, and save-migration checks (`node tools/balance.js --saves`). Every phase in PLAN.md §4 is built. Phase 15 (visual detail) then repainted the world after reference shots the user supplied: a split view with a bright waterline, painted mountains, forests and shores (`js/gfx/paint.js`), light that changes with the hour, a larger boat, fisher and dog, and detailed landmarks and underwater scenes. **Since then the game has moved to a native 640×360 view** (the user's choice, replacing §0.9's 320×180; see PLAN.md §8.3 #82–94), after Cast n Chill screenshots the user supplied, under strict pixel-art rules (one pixel size, flat colour clusters, no dithering or random speckle; §8.3 #84). Phase 16 and 17b redrew the bait shop after the Cast n Chill shop, with an original old shopkeeper, Rusty, worn down to the game's mood; Phase 17 added the 640×360 base, a fine bitmap font, painted fish portraits (`fishgen.portrait`), and repainted the journal (an open book) and the cabin (a log room with a fireplace and five restoration stages); Phase 18 repainted the four waters, the boat, the fisher, the dog in all its poses, and the HUD, catch card, map, pause and tape. Phase 19 repainted the underwater view (the bed, boulders, snags, weed, far rocks, light shafts, the lantern's cone, the creek's current) and made the fish native swimmers with a tail flick and a glitch shimmer, and moved `?gallery` to 640×360. **The whole game now draws at 640×360.** The realism Phases 20–26 followed ([PLAN_REALISM.md](PLAN_REALISM.md)), and Phase 27 began the fish-swimming plan ([PLAN_FISHSWIM.md](PLAN_FISHSWIM.md)): fish beat their tails as seen from the side, turn round through angled frames and pitch as they rise and dive. Phase 28 made the fish simulation steer: a heading and a speed with limited turning, bursts and glides, and in the creek fish that face into the current. Phase 29 made the hooked fish swim: slow boring runs that dive while it is fresh, head shakes drawn on its body, and a tired fish rolling onto its side. Phase 30 turned the far silhouettes into loose shoals that dart away from splashes and the passing boat, then regroup. **The rod-and-fisher plan ([PLAN_ROD.md](PLAN_ROD.md), Phases 31–35)** answers the user's ask for a rod that bends like a real one and for idle animations (preparing the lure, opening a beer). Phase 31 made the rod a tapered beam: the line's load, with its direction, bends it progressively (the tip first, then down into the butt), the tip turns toward the fish, the rod tier sets its action (slow to fast), and it is drawn with a cork butt, reel seat, reel, guides and the line running through them. Phase 31b fixed it after the user saw it bend both ways under tension: the old solver could settle on shapes bent backward, the rod had no inertia, and its tip did not follow the line. Now a stable solver keeps one smooth curve that springs and settles, a stiffer, more tapered blank lets the tip follow a tight line, the load reaches the rod only through a tight line, the waiting line settles into a light belly, a bite takes up the slack and then tugs, and `node tools/balance.js --rod` tests all of it. Phase 32 put the rod in motion: the fisher's hands move between poses instead of jumping, the cast bends the rod by inertia (the tip lags on the stroke, whips past when it stops, and springs back), head shakes knock the tip, and the reel's handle turns while you reel, with the bail open for the cast. Phase 33 made the fisher posable (`js/scene/fisher.js`): his arms are painted for wherever his hands are, they ease between poses, the rod can rest in a holder on the gunwale, and an idle director plays short acts when nothing happens (the thermos between casts, a rest with the rod in the holder while waiting), ending them the moment a key is pressed or a fish bites. Phase 34 hung the lure from the rod tip between casts (it swings as the boat starts and stops) and added the act of preparing it: the rod upright in the holder, line let out until the lure hangs before his face, a look, fresh bait worked on, a tug on the knot, and the lure reeled back up under the tip, with its ticks and reel clicks. Phase 35 gave him a cooler and a beer at dusk and at night: he cracks a can, drinks, sets it on the gunwale by his hip, comes back to it for a sip, and puts the empty back in the cooler; in the grey hours he still drinks from the thermos. A bite while he drinks makes him set the can down and take the rod. The rod-and-fisher plan is complete. **The bait-shop plan ([PLAN_SHOP.md](PLAN_SHOP.md), Phases 36–40)** turns the shop into a small room you walk into, as in a retro Pokémon game, in the lake's look. Phase 36 built the room (`js/ui/shop_room.js`): a log room seen from above, floating in black, with windows onto the lake, a rod rack, shelves, the bait along the wall, a counter, a rug and a door; the fisher walks it tile by tile and the dog follows a tile behind; Phase 37 put Rusty behind the counter, top-down, reading his paper and sipping his coffee; talking to him opens a Pokémon-style text box along the bottom that types his line of the visit, then Buy, Talk or Leave; Buy opens an item window (the list with icons and prices, the coins, the chosen item's effect in the box). The old painted shop and its panel are gone. Phase 38 made the shelves themselves the shop: face the rod rack, the equipment shelf, the worm box, the cricket crate, the minnow tank or the jar and Space opens just their items; rods you own leave the rack, the lantern and the finder leave their shelf, and the tank and the crate empty a little with each pack bought. Phase 39 gave the shop the lake's hour and mood: its windows show the lake's sky, far shore, water, fog and drizzle at that hour; at night the room sinks into dark planks around a hanging lamp; dust drifts in its light, the tank bubbles, Rusty's cup steams, the clock keeps the game's time; with unease, quietly, the clock runs back, a pale figure stands on the far shore, or Rusty has his back to you; and it has its sounds (the door's bell, footsteps, the humming third board, the tank's pump, rain on the glass). Phase 40 fitted it in: touch walks and talks there through the existing menu zones, `dbg.shopWalk(path)` walks him by letters, the gallery has a shop page, and the frame costs 0.13 ms. The bait-shop plan is complete. **The tackle, company and living-lake plan ([PLAN_TACKLE.md](PLAN_TACKLE.md), Phases 41–50)** answers the user's next list (stowing the rod and steering, the dog's barks, rods and lures to choose, a setup panel on S, smaller fish, small menus, a choice of fisher and dog, an ecosystem); the user answered its questions (its §4). Phase 41 put the rod in clips on the gunwale and his hand on the tiller whenever the boat is under way, with the cord pulled on a pull-start. Phase 42 gave the dog a voice: a soft synthesised woof now and then (every 3–8 minutes) and at a fish jumping near the boat, a big catch or the shop's bell, never while a fish bites, and heard at the legendary spots too; P → *Dog barks* turns it off. Phase 43 made rods a rack you own (each with a power and an action; the new Ultralight Wand draws small fish, heavy rods tear small mouths) and added five lures — a spinner, a minnow plug, a soft grub, a popper and a jig — that fish their own depths and move their own way, can be lost when the line snaps and run out, and that each species takes or ignores (an ignoring fish swims past). The shop's rack sells and switches rods, and a lure case sells the lures. Phase 44 added smaller fish: ten small species (3–10 cm) in the shallows, two or three to a water, drawn at their size; every species now runs mostly small with a few big ones; a fry barely bends the long rod and bends the wand well. Phase 46 made the menus small: one shared panel (`js/ui/panel.js`: night ink, a paper rim, a title tab, key caps) for a compact catch card at the top centre (the fish, its size, value and NEW!, SELL and KEEP as keys you can tap), the pause list at the right edge, the tape, the map's place card and the shop's item window, with the world no longer dimmed behind them. ## Architecture

The tree follows PLAN.md §3. These files exist so far:

```
index.html           canvas, script load order (core → data → sim → gfx → scene → ui), boot
js/core/             debug.js rng.js state.js input.js save.js loop.js
data/                loc_fog.js loc_creek.js loc_pines.js loc_red.js fish.js gear.js dialogue.js anomalies.js
js/sim/              clock.js unease.js fish_ai.js bite.js fight.js economy.js passive.js   (pure; run in Node)
js/gfx/              bake.js sprites.js paint.js fx.js water.js surface.js rope.js rod.js fishgen.js
js/scene/            boat.js fisher.js dog.js underwater.js scenery.js sailing.js
js/audio/            engine.js
js/ui/               hud.js panel.js card.js menu.js journal.js shop_room.js shop.js map.js cabin.js tape.js
tools/balance.js     --validate | --replay <seed> | --pacing | --passive | --saves | --fight | --rod | --tackle
```

- **Realism phases (20–25, [PLAN_REALISM.md](PLAN_REALISM.md)):** `G.gfx.surface` is a height field along the water in view pixels (swell from `loc.swell`, ripples from `poke(x, s, width)`, the boat's wake from `wake(...)`); the waterline, the mirror's row shifts, the caustics and everything that floats read it (`sailing.floatY(x)`). `G.audio.engine` (Phase 25) builds a sound kit at unlock and has a water bus (muffled under water, with a reverb per water) and `meter()`. `G.gfx.rope` is the line: `sailing.ropeStep` sets its anchors and rest length by state every update, and `drawRope` draws it. `underwater.flow(loc, depth)` is the current now, from the same `loc.drift` as the lure; the rope, the weeds, motes, bubbles and streaks use it, and `underwater.bodyStep`/`drawLure` give the lure its heading and bait sprite.
- **Fish swimming (27, [PLAN_FISHSWIM.md](PLAN_FISHSWIM.md)):** `fishgen` paints each swimmer in frames `FR` (rest, tail away, glitch, tail near, two turn frames) and `BEAT` is the tail's cycle. `underwater.swimStep` (called in `sailing.update` before `ropeStep`) steps a body per school slot and for the hooked fish: beat phase, glide, facing through turns, pitch. `drawFish`, `drawHooked` and `mouth()` only read the bodies; `drawSwimmer(ctx, cv, x, y, slope, face)` draws a pitched fish as sheared strips. Since Phase 28 the sim steers each fish (`fish_ai.M`, `swim()`, `hover()`; fields `yaw`, `pitch`, `spd`, `gl`, `face`), the scene gives it the current through `school.ctx.flowAt`, and the bodies take their facing, beat, glide and pitch from it. The hooked fish's body (Phase 29) has modes: swimming, shaking (`FR.SHAKE`, with a head `jerk`) and rolled (`FR.ROLL`); `underwater.HOOK` holds its constants. The far shoals (Phase 30) are `underwater.SB` and `shoalStep`/`drawShoals`; `fx.listen(fn)` tells them about splashes; `underwater.shoals()` lists them for checks.

- **The rod (31, 31b, [PLAN_ROD.md](PLAN_ROD.md)):** `G.gfx.rod` is the blank as a beam (`solve(angle, load, dirX, dirY, facing, tier, dt)`, drawn joints in `jx`/`jy`, local x forward and y up from the hands). It keeps the settled shape for the present load (`q`) and the drawn shape (`d`), which springs toward it at the blank's first mode, and lags the load and the line's direction by the line's stretch. `sailing.rodPose` gives `[angle, load, pull]` by state and `rodStep` (in `update`, before `ropeStep`) scales the load by how straight the line is and solves it with the line's direction from rope point 3. Since Phase 32 the hands ease toward the scene's angle (exactly, `direct`, for the cast), their angular acceleration bends the blank by inertia (`hang`: the lure's mass while it hangs at the tip), `rod.kick` knocks it, and `rod.reel` turns the handle and sets the bail; `boat.drawReel` draws the reel after the hull. **The fisher (33):** `G.scene.fisher` holds his state (`run.fisher`): hands, pose, head, item, where the rod is, and the idle director (`step`, `start`, `abort`, acts in `ACTS`); `boat.draw` calls `fisher.draw` and `drawHolder`, and `boat.rodBase` reads `fisher.rodLocal`. `boat.rodTip(b, cx, wy, out)` is the bent tip, `boat.rodBase` the hands; `boat.draw` reads the rod and draws its parts.

- **The view (640×360):** `G.core.view` in `core/loop.js`. A scene or overlay with `native: true` (shop, cabin, journal, map, pause, tape) draws in the view's pixels; the sailing scene still works in world pixels (the sim's 320×180 units) under a ×2 transform, but bakes every image at `HI` = 2 and draws it at half size, and draws live things in half pixels, so it is native too. The HUD and the catch card switch the view to native for themselves. `underwater.js` works the same way since Phase 19, and its fish are `fishgen.swimmer` canvases. Text at 640×360 uses `G.gfx.sprites.fine` (`drawText`, `bold`, `measure`, `wrap`); painted art uses `G.gfx.paint` (`area`, `tone`, `blob`, `limb`, `line`, `slab`, `box`).

- **Flow:** the scene manager holds `sailing`, `shop`, `cabin` and `gallery`. Each loop step first gives the overlays (`G.ui.menu`: pause, journal, map, tape) the step; while one is open the scene waits. `sailing.js` is the scene in play: the passive toggle, clock, unease, anomaly scheduler, owl, cast (with the creek's drift), fish school, the passive cycle, the location's scenery module, then `underwater.catchStep` (bite → hook → fight → landing → card), dog cues. The map is the way into the shop and the cabin.
- **State:** `G.state.save` is saved. `G.state.run` is never saved and holds the boat, camera, cast (with its spot boost), school, fight, hook, the active anomaly, the owl, the spots as they stand (the Red Room moves them), the scenery module's state, passive mode and so on. The boot script fishes time away (`G.ui.tape.away`) before the first frame.
- **Saves:** localStorage key `softstatic_save_v1`, save format version 4 (migrations from v1). They are stored per browser, so they do not travel with git.
- **Keys:** in the bait shop W/A/S/D or arrows walk, Space talks to what he faces (Phase 36); in its text box and windows W/S choose, Space goes on or buys, Esc steps back (Phase 37). A/D or arrows sail, Space (or mouse) casts and reels, W/S or ↑/↓ move in menus, Enter confirms, Esc goes back, P pauses, J journal, M map, TAB passive. URL flags: `?debug`, `?seed=N`, `?gallery`, `?compact`.

## How to continue on another computer

1. **Clone** the repository: `git clone https://github.com/serek9/SoftStatic.git`.
2. **Install Node** (any recent LTS; developed on v26) and **Python 3** to serve the files. VS Code Live Server works too. No `npm install` is needed: there are no dependencies.
3. **Run the game:** `python3 -m http.server 5517 --bind 127.0.0.1` (on Windows, `python`), then open `http://127.0.0.1:5517/?debug`.
4. **Check that everything works:** `node tools/balance.js --validate`, `node tools/balance.js --replay 12345` (hash `82118876` since Phase 28, unchanged by Phases 31 and 31b), `node tools/balance.js --pacing`, `node tools/balance.js --passive`, `node tools/balance.js --fight`, `node tools/balance.js --saves`, `node tools/balance.js --rod`. All should end with OK or PASS and exit 0 (expected output in PLAN.md §8.5).
5. **Claude Code:** open the folder and Claude will read [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). To resume, read PLAN.md §8.6 (the next phase of [PLAN_ROD.md](PLAN_ROD.md)). Visual work is checked with screenshots: in a cloud session, Playwright's Chromium against the local server (`?debug&seed=N`, then `dbg.hour(h)`, `dbg.tp(x)`, `dbg.ui(name)`, `G.scene.sailing.load(id)`). Claude's memory and settings are per machine and are not in git; everything it needs is in PLAN.md, CLAUDE.md and this file.
6. **Moving a save:** saves stay in each browser (and only one tab plays a save at a time; an older tab waits until clicked). In the game, press P, choose **Export save** (it copies the save string, or shows it to copy), and on the other machine choose **Import save** and paste it (between casts). The console equivalents are `G.core.save.exportString(G.state.save)` and `G.ui.menu.importSave(s)`.

**Portability notes:** line endings are fixed to LF by `.gitattributes`, so `core.autocrlf` settings don't matter. All script paths in `index.html` match the files' case exactly, which matters on case-sensitive file systems. The code has no absolute paths. `tools/balance.js` builds paths with `path.join`.

## Decisions and departures from the plan

The full, numbered list is PLAN.md §8.3 (228 entries). The ones most likely to surprise:

- **640×360 instead of 320×180** (user's decision, 2026-10-29): the sim still uses 320×180 world pixels; only drawing changed, so the replay hash and balance did not move.
- **Pixel-art rules for new art** (§8.3 #84): no `ctx.scale` of art (except the sailing scene's half-size drawing of 2× bakes), no dithering, no random speckle, 3–6 flat keys per material, light that steps with planks or logs.
- **Realism phases (20–25)** were asked for by the user and planned in [PLAN_REALISM.md](PLAN_REALISM.md), with five approved spec deviations (R1–R5): new files `surface.js` and `rope.js`, a fight that can be lost on a slack line, a simulated line instead of a vertical dotted one, a current on every water, and an optional `jumps` field on fish.
- **The rope never moves the lure:** the lure's path is the sim's, and the rope is pinned to where the lure is drawn, so the replay hash did not change. Reeling takes up the slack fast instead of delaying the lure.
- **Every water has a current now** (the lakes a slow wind drift, one way per cast since Phase 26; the Red Room one that turns round every 23 s). The live game's lure drifts; the balance models do not, so the baselines did not move. A lure that drifts into a spot gains its boost.
- **Fish can get away on a slack line** (Phase 23, a change to V4 approved by the user): head shakes and jumps work the hook loose when tension is under 0.12. A careful player lands about 97%, a careless one about 70%; a loss is quiet, never a fail screen. This moved the replay hash and the pacing; `--pacing` now uses 20 seeds because 5 were too noisy, and passive's `incomeMult` is 0.63.
- **The boat pitches by shearing, not rotating** (Phase 24), so the pixel art stays crisp. Its own wake is a wave layer in the surface whose waves travel at the boat's top speed, so they roll under it when it brakes (Phase 26; at first it was scripted). It moves only once its motor runs.
- **Fish swimming is presentation first** (Phase 27): the drawn fish's beat, turns and pitch come from bodies the scene steps beside the sim, so the sim and the baselines did not move. Phase 28 then changed the sim's own movement (heading, burst and glide, the current), which moved the replay hash and the pacing medians (all still in their bands; the creek's shift is the reshuffled random stream, PLAN.md §8.3 #145). Fish bursts take their lengths from a hash, not the rng. A silhouette is now the colour frame filled flat, so each frame is painted once.
- **The rod bends from a load, not a script** (Phase 31): the old `bend` number per state is now a load (0–1 of the rod's rating) with a direction, the line's, and the blank's taper decides where it bends. The tip moves (it used to stay put), so the rope, the lure's flick and the draw all read one bent tip. The fight holds the rod higher than before, since the bend brings the tip down. Since Phase 31b the rod keeps a settled shape and a drawn one that springs toward it, so it never bends backward or into an S, and a pull reaches it only through a tight line. Since Phase 32 the cast's bend is the rod's inertia, not a script, and the fisher's hands ease between angles. Presentation only: the baselines did not move.
- **Every fishing sound is synthesised** (Phase 25; no sound files, §0.2): the kit is computed into buffers at the first key press (about 70–85 ms). Scenes tell the engine about one-off events with `G.audio.engine.cue(...)`; everything else it still reads from state.
- **Rusty** is an original shopkeeper in the spirit of the reference, not a copy; the shop's lines are still `data/dialogue.js`. Since Phase 37 he is drawn top-down behind the counter of the walkable shop and speaks in a text box.

- **Debug overlay and red errors are page text**, not canvas text. Save export/import also uses the browser's own prompt dialog when the clipboard is unavailable.
- **No fail states:** extra state-machine exits back to `sailing` (cancel a charge, reel in early, snap, escape).
- **Save format:** `SS1.<base64>.<checksum>`, for storage and export. A corrupt save is kept under `softstatic_save_v1_corrupt` and the game starts fresh.
- **Overlays freeze the world:** while the pause menu, journal or map is open, nothing moves, including the clock.
- **The map is the hub:** the bait shop and the cabin are reached from it; M works only from the boat with no line out.
- **Shopkeeper lines are chosen without randomness;** each special line is said once per save.
- **Anomalies run only in the game, not in the balance sessions,** so the replay hash and pacing are unaffected by them. Their cooldowns live in run state and reset on reload.
- **Where a cast lands matters:** an anomaly or a spot under the landing point boosts that cast until it is reeled in.
- **Journal hints are discovered by catching** (hint lines on the first catch, bait on the second, depth and hours on the third), and late hints arrive one per new catch at unease 0.4 or more, in blue pen.
- **Trophies that face the door** are decided only on entering the cabin, from the unease of the water you came from.
- **Location costs and conditions live in each `data/loc_*.js`** (`cost`, `need`); `data/gear.js` no longer lists them.
- **Scenery modules** (`js/scene/scenery.js`, a file not in the §3 tree) draw what baked layers cannot: the Pines' signal, neon and lamps, the Red Room's moving structures and crimson reflection. Fogbound and the creek have none.
- **The Red Room's structures move after each cast lands** (seeded by that cast), each carrying a spot; the boost goes by where they stood when the lure landed.
- **Passive mode is the same cycle everywhere:** live (TAB) and time away both run `sim/passive.js` plan/apply, so they earn alike. `incomeMult` (0.6) is the only knob for the share; the wait `base` (48 s) is set so a cycle matches an active cast before it.
- **Time away moves the game clock** (it used to resume where it was saved), and on boot the tape shows what happened.
- **A phantom journal entry never counts as caught** until you really catch that fish.
- **One tab owns the save:** opening the game in a second tab (or `?compact`) makes the first one wait, so they never overwrite each other.
- **Pacing model:** it moves to each water it opens and hunts the Pines' key in the 03:33 window (task 14.5).
